# M4: Everything else in daily use (L)
**Goal:** feature parity for the rest of 6.1.

**Work:**
1. **History:** one timeline of seizures and between-seizures entries, as a list, and as a **graphical timeline view** (days or weeks on a zoomable axis; seizures sized by duration and coloured by type; between-seizures entries, medication changes and missed doses as markers; tap anything to open it). The list keeps filters (type, dates, needs details), trend chart, event detail with the vertical seizure timeline (README section 5.1), danger flags against the patient's rule, the audit trail, delete (tombstone), and navigation that keeps filters.
2. **Medications:**
   - active ingredient and form pickers
   - schedules with per-slot doses
   - change history (started, changed, stopped, correction) with effective date and dose time, and confirm sheets
   - stop instead of delete, rename reasons
   - dose panel and extra dose
   - missed and assumed doses
   - history grid
   - reminders for every patient
3. **Wellbeing (optional module, off by default, decided 2026-10-30):**
   - check-in, factors (editable definitions), history and correlation view, daily reminder
   - switched on in Settings; while off, nothing of it appears anywhere (no home card, tab, reminder or report section)
   - built last in M4, and may move to a 7.x release if time is tight
4. **EEG diary:** sessions, activities, seizure references, history.
5. **Video:** record during a seizure, linked to the seizure; it stays on the device that recorded it.
6. **Patients:** add, rename, colour, remove; the switcher; "Recording for".
7. **Settings:** every 6.1 setting group rebuilt (identity, medications, appearance, recording and emergency plan, clinician, data and backup), plus app lock and storage status.
8. **Help, About, What's new, install guidance, update prompt, backup reminder.**

**Done when:**
- Every remaining checklist item, except reports and household, passes.
- The audit passes on every screen.
- You've used v7 alongside 6.1 for a few days.
