# M6: Household without a hub, then the 7.0 release (L)
**Goal:** registered devices keep each other current without any server.

**Work:**
1. **Household:** create, invite by QR and PIN, device names, follow patients, remove a device (key replaced for future data), the recovery kit.
2. **Sync by meeting:**
   - Devices find each other through the public PeerJS broker over the internet, or directly on the same Wi-Fi.
   - Every open device advertises itself; on opening, a device looks for others for about a minute.
   - They exchange the change-log entries the other lacks, so devices pass on what they got from others.
   - The recording device stays reachable a little longer after a save.
   - Background sync on installed Android.
   - A "Household up to date?" screen.
   - Encrypted update by QR code or file as a last resort.
3. **Conflict handling** per merge rule, with a quiet note of what changed and from which device.
4. **Multi-device tests:** three devices meeting in different orders, offline edits, deletions, removed devices.
5. **Release 7.0:**
   - final audit and performance budgets
   - translation review (at least Norwegian)
   - your real-device check
   - switch over from 6.1 with the latest backup

**Done when:**
- All checklist items pass and the multi-device tests are green.
- You've used two or more of your devices together for some days.
- 7.0 is tagged.
