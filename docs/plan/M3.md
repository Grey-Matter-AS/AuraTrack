# M3: Recording and the new logging (L)
**Goal:** the core of AuraTrack, faster and easier than 6.1.

**Work:**
1. **Clickable prototype first:** home, recording, the tabbed seizure editor (Before, During, After, Details, Review) with the vertical graphical timeline (README section 5.1) and the body map, the between-seizures log, and the ILAE questions, built from kit components with fake data. **You try it on your phone. Changes are agreed before the real build.**
2. **Home:** Start Timer and Log Past Seizure (6.1's two circles), recent seizures, dose panel and wellbeing card placeholders for M4, and the patient chip.
3. **Recording:**
   - The seizure is saved the moment Start is tapped (in progress), and every phase tap and symptom is written at once.
   - Phase tiles as in 6.1: End aura, End seizure; "no aura" by tapping End seizure first.
   - Emergency plan alert at the user's set time, measuring seizure time; a warning one minute earlier.
   - 12-minute auto-stop.
   - The optional symptom strip (usual symptoms, one tap, timed automatically).
   - Quick notes, video, wake lock, haptics.
   - Crash recovery.
   - Start works from the lock screen, with what the locked alert shows set in settings.
4. **Seizure editor, organised in tabs by phase (decided 2026-10-30).** Used after recording, for past seizures and for edits. Each tab shows only its own part, so the screen stays calm, but **nothing is hidden**:
   - **The tabs, always visible:**
     - **Before** (prodrome and aura: warning signs, aura symptoms)
     - **During** (the seizure itself)
     - **After** (post-ictal findings, recovery time)
     - **Details** (seizure type and ILAE questions, triggers, notes, phase times)
   - **Each tab shows its state:** a count of what's been added, or "not filled in yet". The order follows the seizure: Before → During → After → Details.
   - **Inside each tab:**
     - **That phase's frequent items first:** saved sets for this seizure type, then "most used for this type in this phase". Then a search over the full symptom list.
     - **Body map** for where it was felt or seen: front, back, head close-up, left/right, several areas at once, plus one-tap "whole body", "both sides" and "started one side, spread".
     - **Order within the phase:** on the timeline (below): drag, or move up and down; the time since the previous moment, exact or approximate. The first symptom of the seizure is clearly marked.
   - **Overview, at the top of every tab:** a compact strip of the whole seizure across the phases (aura → seizure → recovery, with times), so you always see the full picture and can jump to any phase.
   - **Graphical seizure timeline (vertical, README section 5.1):**
     - a vertical line with the phases in their colours (aura amber, seizure red, recovery blue) and phase markers with times (start, aura ends, seizure ends, back to normal)
     - **moments** on the line, each holding one or more symptoms as rounded boxes on one shared leader line; drag a box onto another moment to make them simultaneous, or off to separate them
     - spacing by order, not true scale; the interval between moments is written on the line and chosen from: same time, a few seconds, about 10 s, about 30 s, about 1 min, custom, unknown; stored as exact (tapped live), approximate ("~") or unknown
     - the ★ first symptom of the seizure; boxes show name, body area or side, and "~" when approximate
     - prodrome hours before as a broken-line section ("≈ 2 h before")
     - a "+" between moments inserts one; drag or move up and down; a list view with the same content for screen readers
     - phones: line on the left, boxes on the right; wider screens: boxes alternate sides; the layout audit checks boxes and leader lines never overlap
     - one component: compact in the overview strip, full in Review, and reused on the detail page (M4) and in the PDF (M5)
     - data: each moment has a phase, an offset from the seizure start, a precision and its symptom codes; intervals are derived from the offsets
   - **Review before saving:** the full timeline in order across all phases, each item tappable to edit. Save works from any tab; it never requires visiting every tab.
   - **During recording,** symptoms tapped in the optional symptom strip land in the right phase automatically (the phase running at that moment).
   - Phase times are editable in Details, with their source shown (timed, edited, entered later).
5. **Saved symptom sets and custom symptoms** (the 6.1 behaviour, on codes), managed in settings. Sets now remember which phase each symptom belongs to.
5b. **"Between seizures" log (interictal, decided 2026-10-30):** a quick entry from the home screen for symptoms between seizures (for example myoclonic jerks, absences noticed later, headaches, memory or mood changes): symptom, time, body area, note. Shown on the same timeline as seizures in History (M4) and in the neurologist report (M5).
6. **App lock:** PIN, plus a passkey where supported, and the lock screen with Start.

**Done when:**
- Every checklist item for recording, tagging and logging passes its end-to-end test.
- The audit passes on these screens.
- Start reacts in under 100 ms on a mid-range phone.
- You've recorded test seizures on your devices.
