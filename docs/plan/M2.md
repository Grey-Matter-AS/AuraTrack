# M2: Design system and layout guarantee (M)
**Goal:** every building block of the interface, identical everywhere, with the no-overlap rule enforced by machine.

**Work:**
1. **`packages/ui`, all typed, accessible and themed:**
   - Button, IconButton, Toggle, ChipGroup, ChoiceList
   - TextField, NumberField (fresh entry), DecimalField
   - DateTimeField (own hint and icon)
   - Select (never blank)
   - Checkbox
   - FieldRow
   - Section, Card, Inset, EmptyState
   - BottomSheet, Dialog, Toast (focus moves in, is trapped, and returns; Escape; only the topmost reacts)
   - Tabs (chips on wide screens, a picker when narrow)
   - the screen-stack navigator
   - a header with the patient chip
   - reduced motion
2. **Layout primitives:** fluid type, container queries, safe areas, one overlay layer with z-order rules, scroll areas whose children never shrink.
3. **Lint rules:** screens may only use kit components and kit overlays; no raw dates from `toISOString`.
4. **Overlap and contrast audit** as a permanent end-to-end test:
   - a "kitchen sink" page showing every component in every state
   - the device matrix (small iPhone, iPhone 15, small Android, Pixel, iPad portrait and landscape, Android tablet, laptop, phone landscape)
   - dark and light, all accents, all text sizes
   - checks: overlap, clipping, offscreen, tap targets of at least 44px, contrast of at least 4.5:1
5. **Visual baseline screenshots** of the kitchen sink.

**Done when:**
- The audit passes on the whole matrix.
- Component tests pass for every component.
- You've looked at the kitchen sink on your own phones.
