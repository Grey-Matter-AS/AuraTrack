# M1: Data core and import (L)
**Goal:** everything that stores, protects and merges data, tested to the bone, plus your 6.1 history imported.

**Work:**
1. **`packages/model`:** zod schemas for:
   - household, device, patient
   - seizure (phase marks with source, time zone, timeline symptoms with order and time, body areas, ILAE classification, after-seizure findings, triggers, notes, video reference)
   - medication and medication change, dose log
   - wellbeing entry, EEG session and activity
   - settings (device, patient, household)
   - change-log entry and tombstone
2. **Codes catalogue (versioned):**
   - seizure types and the ILAE parts
   - symptoms, from 6.1's symptom wizard turned into codes and grouped
   - body areas: map regions, sides and close-ups
   - triggers, after-seizure findings and moods (6.1's lists as codes)
   - custom codes per household

   English labels go in `packages/i18n`; the other 8 languages are ported from 6.1 where the meaning maps exactly, and flagged for review where it doesn't.
3. **Clinical rules ported with their 6.1 tests:** emergency plan (alert time, cluster rule, seizure-time measurement), 12-minute auto-stop, medication regimen in force at a time, scheduled doses, missed and assumed doses.
4. **`packages/store`:**
   - IndexedDB through Dexie
   - every record encrypted with AES-GCM, under a device key created with WebCrypto
   - the key wrapped by a PIN-derived key (Argon2id, or PBKDF2 as a fallback), or by a passkey where PRF is supported
   - the in-memory decrypted working set
   - the change log with HLC timestamps, per field
   - tombstones
   - per-record-type merge rules: newest edit per field wins; dose status rank (taken beats assumed beats missed); medication changes and the audit trail are only ever added to
   - persistent-storage request and an installed-PWA check
5. **Web Worker host:** crypto, merge and import run off the main thread, with a typed message interface.
6. **Backup v7:** an encrypted file with every patient and every record, verified on restore (counts per table, rejected records listed), plus the recovery kit (a printable QR of the household keys).
7. **6.1 importer:**
   - reads plain and encrypted 6.1 backups, single-patient and all-patients
   - maps English labels to codes, with a review list for anything unmapped (kept as custom)
   - laps and manual durations → phase marks
   - post-ictal findings → codes
   - medication history → change log
8. **An import comparison report:** seizures per month, durations, medication changes and doses compared with 6.1's own numbers.

**Done when:**
- Unit, property and mutation targets are met for model, store and import.
- Your real 6.1 backup imports with zero lost records and a comparison report you've checked.
- Not yet any real screens: a developer page lists the imported data.
