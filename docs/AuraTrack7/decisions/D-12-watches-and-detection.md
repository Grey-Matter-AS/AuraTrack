# D-12: Watches with the phones; detection early and hidden

**Decided:** 2026-11-03, by you, in the native-apps discussion. Part of `../ROADMAP.md`.

## Decision
Apple Watch and Wear OS apps are built together with the phone apps (N9). Seizure detection (generalized tonic-clonic) is built beside them: monitoring whenever the wearer chooses; a countdown on the wrist; if not cancelled, **a recording starts automatically, counting from the estimated onset** (marked approximate); carers alerted; cancelled detections kept as false alarms. Hidden in released apps until you confirm regulatory approval, then off by default.

## Why
Your decision after discussion. Starting from the estimated onset errs on the safe side: the 5-minute alert comes earliest. This changes how a recording can start; the clinical rules themselves (5-minute alert, 12-minute stop, no extra buttons) are unchanged.
