# D-7: GPL-3.0 with an app-store exception, and a name policy

**Decided:** 2026-11-03, by you, in the native-apps discussion. Part of `../ROADMAP.md`.

## Decision
GPL-3.0-or-later plus a GPLv3 §7 additional permission for app-store distribution, added while you hold all copyright. A §7(e) term declines rights to the names AuraTrack and AuraTrack7 and the logo; `TRADEMARKS.md` states that forks must rename. The names are used with ™. Registration (Patentstyret or EUIPO, fee) is optional and later.

## Why
The repository is already GPL-3.0. Apple's store terms conflict with plain GPL once others contribute; the exception settles that. No licence can keep forks free of charge or ads; the name policy stops such copies from calling themselves AuraTrack7.
