# D-8: MDR Class I, self-declared, with its own regulatory track

**Decided:** 2026-11-03, by you, in the native-apps discussion. Part of `../ROADMAP.md`.

## Decision
The apps are treated as a Class I medical device, self-declared. The regulatory file (intended use, ISO 14971 risk file, IEC 62304 lifecycle records, usability, post-market plan, declaration, UDI, EUDAMED) runs alongside every milestone and is finished in N22. Adviser work is yours. Health-data statistics and detection are built; detection stays hidden until you confirm approval.

## Why
Your adviser's conclusion. Every release goes through change control; the test suite and sign-off files are the verification evidence.
